## Returns

For eligible online purchases, you can tell us you wish to cancel within 14 days after receiving your order. You then have a further 14 days to send the items back. Sale items have the same statutory return rights.

Contact us on WhatsApp at +44 7405 859821 with your order number and the items you wish to return. We will provide return instructions. Contacting us does not replace or limit your statutory cancellation rights.

Please handle items with care and, where possible, return them with their tags and original packaging. You may inspect or try on clothing as you would in a shop. We may deduct for loss of value caused by handling beyond what is necessary to establish an item's nature, characteristics and functioning.
