Returns and refunds

For eligible UK online purchases, you may cancel within 14 days after receiving your order. You then have a further 14 days to return the items. These rights also apply to sale items.

Contact us and return your items

We recommend contacting us first on WhatsApp at +44 7405 859821 or by email at stylish.uk.fashion@gmail.com, quoting your order number. Please get in touch for the correct return address and instructions. Contacting us first does not limit your statutory cancellation rights.

For change-of-mind returns, you pay the return postage. Please keep proof of postage.

You may inspect and try on clothing as you would in a shop. Please take care of items and include tags and packaging where possible. We may deduct for any loss in value caused by handling beyond what is necessary to establish the item's nature, characteristics and functioning.

Refunds

We refund cancelled purchases to the original payment method within 14 days of receiving the returned goods or evidence that you sent them back, whichever is earlier. For a cancelled whole order, standard outbound delivery is also refunded; the extra cost of an upgraded delivery service is not.

Faulty or incorrect items

If an item is faulty, damaged or not as described, please contact us so we can arrange the appropriate remedy and cover reasonable return costs. Your statutory rights are unaffected by this policy.

Exchanges

We use a return-and-reorder process. We recommend getting in touch first so we can check availability and help you choose the right size or colour. Return the original item under this policy and place a new order for the replacement. Replacement purchases are subject to current prices, availability and delivery charges.
